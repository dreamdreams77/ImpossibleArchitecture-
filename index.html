    How space works here

    A room's position on the plan never decides its connections. Only portals do. Each portal may turn your heading by quarter-turns or reflect it, so you can return to a room facing a new direction, or reach a distant room in one step.

    Keys: Delete removes selection · R turns a portal · M mirrors it · Esc clears · Ctrl/Cmd-Z undo.
